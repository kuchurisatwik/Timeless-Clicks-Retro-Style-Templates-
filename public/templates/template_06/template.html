PARIS FASHION
WEEK
HIGHLIGHTS!
By Olivia Brooks
Fashion Correspondent

Paris once again proved why it remains the heart of the fashion world. This season brought a celebration of craftsmanship, innovation, and timeless elegance. From structured tailoring to fluid silhouettes, designers embraced contrast—merging classic heritage with modern expression.


Luxury houses unveiled bold statements in texture and proportion, while emerging designers brought fresh perspectives rooted in sustainability and authenticity. Street style was equally captivating, with minimal tones, oversized layers, and statement accessories dominating the scene.


This season is not just about what you wear, but how you wear it—confident, effortless, and uniquely you.

As we move into a new era of fashion, one thing is clear: style is personal, and the world is watching.
